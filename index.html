Parcel Desk brings marketplace orders, purchases, inventory, and shipping evidence into one workspace running on the operator’s own computer.

This website provides information about Parcel Desk. Access to the working application is managed privately by its operator.

From inbox to order history

Collect the originals

With the account owner’s permission, Parcel Desk imports received messages from a dedicated Gmail inbox and keeps the original emails as evidence.

Connect the details

Recognized eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, and Coach messages help organize orders, purchases, tracking information, and supporting records. Unclear messages stay available for review.

Check the physical work

Record receiving, inventory allocations, packing photos, and carrier receipts alongside order history. A shipping label alone does not confirm carrier handoff.

Gmail access

Read-only, with your permission.

Parcel Desk requests Gmail read-only access to retrieve email content and organize order records. It does not send, delete, label, or mark messages as read. The permission covers the connected mailbox; the import date range is an application filter.

The local application stores imported messages and derived records on the operator’s computer. See the privacy policy for storage, retention, third-party connections, and your controls.
