Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Parcel Desk is a private order, inventory, and evidence management application operated on a local computer. This policy covers that application and this public information website.

Who operates Parcel Desk

Parcel Desk is intended for the operator and a small group of authorized users. For privacy questions, access questions, or deletion requests, authorized users should contact the workspace operator through their existing private contact method. Personal contact details are not listed on this website.

Information accessed and collected

When you connect Gmail, Google asks you to authorize the gmail.readonly permission. Parcel Desk reads the connected account’s email address, message identifiers, and received messages in its configured import range. Original messages can include sender and recipient addresses, subjects, message bodies, dates, and attachments. The permission covers the mailbox, while date and folder exclusions are application filters.

Recognized messages may contain customer names, order numbers, item descriptions, prices, purchase details, delivery addresses, and shipment tracking information. Unrecognized messages in the import range can also be stored as original evidence. Use a dedicated mailbox for the intended order-related mail.

The application also stores records and files that its users add, such as purchases, stock locations, receipt scans, packing photos, notes, and corrections. Google authorization tokens are stored to allow continued access while authorization remains valid.

How information is used

Parcel Desk uses this information to import and deduplicate email evidence, recognize supported order and purchase messages, organize inventory and shipment records, display operational reports, and let authorized users review or correct records. Gmail access is read-only: the application does not send email, modify labels, mark messages as read, or delete mail.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Parcel Desk’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and access

Imported email files, extracted records, uploaded evidence, and authorization tokens are stored on the operator’s computer. Copies may also exist in backups made by the operator. The application does not itself encrypt these stored files or tokens; their protection depends on device, account, disk, backup, and network controls configured by the operator.

Authorized users may access the workspace over networks enabled by the operator. Access to the device, workspace, and backups should be limited to those users. Operational logs and record history may be retained locally for troubleshooting and auditing.

Third-party connections and sharing

The application communicates with Google for sign-in, token refresh, and Gmail retrieval. Product images may be loaded from external image hosts, and product listing lookups may send listing identifiers to the relevant marketplace. Those services can receive normal request information such as an IP address. Original Gmail messages are not uploaded to this public website.

The operator and authorized workspace users can view stored records. Any exports, backups, or onward sharing performed by those users are managed by the operator. Parcel Desk does not provide an advertising or data resale service.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Records are retained until the operator removes them; the application has no automatic retention deadline. Disconnecting Gmail stops future access but does not erase messages already imported or their derived records.

You can revoke Gmail access through Google Account connections. To request deletion of locally stored messages, related records, or authorization tokens, contact the workspace operator through your existing private contact method. The operator must also address any copies in backups. Local deletion does not delete messages from your Gmail mailbox.

This information website

This is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no Gmail sign-in, email import, contact form, advertising, or site analytics added by Parcel Desk. Its hosting provider may process request information such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the application’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Authorized users can direct questions or requests to the workspace operator through their existing private contact method.
